This folder contains results tables of "Openalex_authors_and_years_extraction.R" (found under "scripts" folder) with authors names and publication years of articles in the DCC and Charité articles as well, for datasets that were found in the DCC. Several attempts were made to extract all information, as documented in the script. The folder is mostly internal, with the final files being used in "ds_primary..." and "ds_added..." .qmd files.
